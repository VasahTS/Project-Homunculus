The repetition comes from the Characters tracker’s own rules: today they tell it to copy card and lorebook facts into the sheet. Following Aventuras, the sheet keeps short facts and what the story changes; long lore stays in the card and the lorebook, which the AI already reads. These are starter texts: your installed trackers get them once, only where you did not edit them.

Rule about canon (Scene: Characters prompt)

- Lorebook entries, character cards and cast sheets are canon for who a character is. When one of them gives a fixed fact (age, height, weight, face, hair, eyes, build, species, powers…), copy it exactly, even if the current values say something else…
- Character cards, lorebook entries and cast sheets are canon and the story's AI already reads them. Never copy their text into a field. Keep fixed facts (age, height, species…) consistent with them, in a few words, and correct a value that contradicts them. Write what the story shows or changes.

New rule: people not in the scene

- For anyone not in the scene, only update "In the scene", where they went and their mood. Leave their other values as they are; they are not shown to you.

New rule: cards with several characters

- A card marked as several characters is not a person. Add each of its people by their own name when the story shows them.
