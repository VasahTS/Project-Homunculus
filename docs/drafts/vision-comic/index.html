Pictures for any model, and a comic turned into one card

Two parts from docs/UI-PLAN.md (D-115). Part 1: a vision model describes pictures for a main model that can't see them, like GLM 5.3. Part 2: the Assistant reads a short comic, says what it understood, asks who you are in it, and proposes one shared card. The comic “After Hours” is invented for the draft; its pages are blank panels. Pages, the reading order, “See Description” and the answers can be clicked.
