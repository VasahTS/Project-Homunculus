Neutral look

The real app (browser preview, demo data) with three looks. Quiet is today. A — Mono: black, grey and white, no green, square corners, hairlines. B — Mono grid: A plus the reference's grid: full-width rows in the sidebar, the selected place in solid white, big uppercase titles.

Quiet (today)
B — Mono grid
Right side:
ColorWindow #0D0D0D, panels #131313, lines #262626. Text white / grey / dark grey. The accent is white: the one strong thing on a screen (Send, the selected tab) is a white block with black text. Status colors stay (danger red, warning amber) because they carry meaning; prompt-source colors stay too.
ShapeSquare corners, no glass, no shadows, no glows. Panels are split by 1 px lines instead of floating. Letter avatars lose the green/blue gradient (flat dark grey). The Assistant's orb becomes a white dot.
Not changed yetFonts: the UI stays on the system font; a bold display face (like the reference's titles) would be a bundled font chosen later. The mode switch (Play/Assistant) and the composer still have round corners here: they get the same square treatment in code. Motion comes next, on this look.
