Read Lyra, Kessa and Northern Woods · thought for 9 s ›
Lyra's card says she never leaves the woods, Kessa's says she “travels light and alone” — fine. Clash: Kessa's card has her as an ex-ranger of the same order Lyra belongs to; that becomes their shared past instead of a contradiction. The lorebook has the mill as “abandoned since the flood”: used as the hideout.

Here's the Kit. One thing I had to settle: both cards put them in the same ranger order, so I made that their history — Kessa left it, Lyra stayed. Nothing in their cards was changed.
